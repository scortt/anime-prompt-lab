# Japanese Animation Directors and Key Artists

用于研究动画视觉语言，不仅记录漫画家，也记录动画导演、作画监督、角色设计师。

## 山田尚子 (Naoko Yamada)

Category: Animation Director / Visual Storytelling

Works:
- K-On!
- A Silent Voice
- Liz and the Blue Bird

Visual Language:
- delicate emotional acting
- subtle body language
- realistic gesture and posture
- cinematic framing
- attention to hands, feet and small movements
- soft lighting and atmosphere

Prompt Keywords:
- delicate anime cinematography
- subtle emotional expression
- realistic character acting
- poetic composition
- quiet everyday atmosphere

## Other Animation-focused Artists

### 新海诚 (Makoto Shinkai)
- atmospheric backgrounds
- light and weather effects
- emotional scenery
- cinematic color composition

### 细田守 (Mamoru Hosoda)
- natural character acting
- family and daily life scenes
- expressive movement

### 今敏 (Satoshi Kon)
- surreal composition
- psychological editing
- dream-like transitions

### 庵野秀明 (Hideaki Anno)
- experimental framing

- dramatic composition

### 吉成曜 (Yoh Yoshinari)

### 矶光雄 (Mitsuo Iso)

### 田中雄一 (Yuichi Tanaka)
